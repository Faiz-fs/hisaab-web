## Brand & Style

This design system channels an ambient, futuristic perspective on personal wealth, crafted specifically for modern Indian professionals navigating hyper-fluid personal finances. The aesthetic merges Apple-tier industrial product storytelling with cinematic, speculative fintech interfaces. It rejects the archaic corporate blues, cluttered spreadsheets, and dry banking paradigms typical of consumer financial software in the region.

The emotional arc is deliberate: from transactional anxiety to hyper-controlled financial lucidity. Surfaces feel heavy, crystalline, and physical—yet bathed in digital light.

### Visual Pillars

- **Liquid Crystal & Deep Atmosphere:** Built upon profound, oceanic dark planes where layers are distinguished through variable-opacity frosted panes, subtle perimeter refractions, and calibrated specular borders rather than brute-force drop shadows.
- **Editorial Hyper-Clarity:** Bold, razor-sharp typography communicates financial authority while maintaining high-fashion editorial posture. Financial telemetry is treated with the precision of aircraft cockpit telemetry.
- **Kinetic Voltage:** Electrified accents slice across abyssal darks to denote capital velocity, transactional thresholds, and automated SMS/UPI reconciliation without visual noise.

## Colors

The palette operates on strict contrast ratios against an ultra-dark backdrop, orchestrating ambient atmospheric radiance with sharp focal punches.

### Core Roles

- **Base Canvas (`#0B0B10`):** Pure void dark with subtle deep violet undertones. Used exclusively for root backgrounds and native Android canvas scaffolding.
- **Surface Tier 1 (`#16161D`):** Opaque, muted dark base for cards, bottom sheets, and inactive modules.
- **Liquid Surface Tier 2 (`#1E1E28`):** Elevated frosted core, rendered dynamically with backdrop blurs and subtle white/lime edge refractions.
- **Structural Stroke (`#262633`):** 1px delineator for spatial containment and structural boundary isolation.
- **Electric Lime (`#C6F432`):** Primary signal color. Commands primary calls-to-action, active tracking toggles, high-value net-worth stats, and critical financial momentum markers.
- **Luminous Mint (`#9FFFD0`):** Secondary prosperity signal. Reserved for cash inflows, UPI auto-credit validations, investment yields, and micro-delights.
- **Coral Flare (`#FF6B7A`):** Tertiary delta signal. Dictates debits, recurring debt deductions, burn rates, and spending overshoots.
- **Text Systems:** `#F8F9FA` for high-impact primary numerals and headlines; `#A1A1B5` for metadata, labels, and secondary context.

### Tinted Refractions

Subtle radial gradients of `#C6F432` (at 4–8% opacity) and `#9FFFD0` (at 3–6% opacity) are introduced behind key summary modules to produce an optical lens-flare refraction beneath translucent surfaces.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere: cinematic, technical, and hyper-structured.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules

- **Display & Headings:** `Space Grotesk` carries an intentional mechanical confidence. Tight negative letter spacing is mandatory on scales above 24px to enforce an architectural visual density.
- **Telemetry & Financial Metrics:** `metric-mono-lg` and `metric-mono-sm` rely on tabular lining numerals (`tnum`, `zero`) to prevent horizontal jitter during live account balance updates and automated expense computations.
- **Running Body & Contextual Narrative:** `Inter` handles operational reading duties. Its neutral, clean geometry complements the expressive character of Space Grotesk.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Always set `label-caps` in uppercase with expanded kerning (`+0.08em`) to act as visual metadata markers above monetary groups.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy prioritizes handheld ergonomic flow on modern Android form factors while adapting to tablet dashboards.

### Fluid Grid Architecture

- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with dynamic safe margins (`margin: 1rem`) and dense `gutter: 1rem`. Focuses on single-column stacks with edge-to-edge liquid glass swipe carousels.
- **Foldable & Tablet (600px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Spans financial breakdown trees and contextual analytics panes side-by-side with `gutter-tablet: 1.5rem`.
- **Desktop / Large Dashboard (1024px+):** 12-column bounded grid (max-width `1280px`), centered, using `margin-desktop: 3rem`.

### Vertical Rhythms & Gaps

Every spacing increment adheres strictly to an 8pt layout cadence with a 4pt sub-grid for tight chip metrics. Component internals use `space-md` (`16px`) for primary padding, while cards maintain a breathing room of `space-lg` (`24px`) vertically to emphasize object boundaries against deep canvas darks.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial separation relies on optical physics—translucency, chromatic dispersion, and structural perimeter lighting—rather than legacy dark dropshadows.

### The Liquid Glass Tiering System

1. **The Oceanic Base (Elevation 0):** Solid `#0B0B10`. Absorbs light; houses ambient wallpaper gradients and global navigation foundations.
2. **Structural Card Tier (Elevation 1):** Solid `#16161D` with a crisp `1px` outer border in `#262633`. Serves as the substrate for standard non-interactive transaction rows.
3. **Liquid Glass Surface (Elevation 2):** Translucent background `rgba(30, 30, 40, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`. Outlined with a top-weighted gradient border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` fading to `rgba(38, 38, 51, 0.4)` at the bottom edge), mimicking a bevel edge catching light.
4. **Active Hero Modals & Overlays (Elevation 3):** `rgba(30, 30, 40, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(36px)`. Border receives a concentrated refractive rim of `rgba(198, 244, 50, 0.3)`. Emits a deep ambient glow: `0 20px 50px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

### Chromatic Light Refractions

Key financial signals cast faint localized glows. For net gains or windfalls, a subtle background glow of `#9FFFD0` at 5% opacity diffuses over a 64px radius behind the balance panel. Expense spikes produce an identical diffuse shadow using `#FF6B7A`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness model (`level 2`) to echo contemporary industrial hardware: softened corner radii that feel ergonomic in the palm, without straying into playful bubble aesthetics.

### Boundary Rules

- **Base Surfaces & Cards:** Standardize at `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to ensure content panels appear grounded and crystalline.
- **Hero Containers & Bottom Sheets:** Utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) at top junctions, wrapping interactive sheets smoothly around the native Android gesture bar.
- **Micro-Controls & Segment Toggles:** Use `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) for tight structural chips and inputs.
- **Action Pills:** Primary triggers and transactional status pills break the rule via continuous organic squircle pill-capsules (`9999px`) to maintain immediate visual affordance.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Electric Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#C6F432`, foreground `#0B0B10`. Label set in `Space Grotesk` medium/bold. On active press, scale interpolates to `0.97` with a localized luminescence flare.
- **Glass Secondary:** Translucent `#1E1E28` surface, 1px border `#262633`, text `#F8F9FA`. On hover/touch: border shifts to `rgba(198, 244, 50, 0.4)`.
- **Destructive/Debit Action:** Background `rgba(255, 107, 122, 0.1)`, 1px border `rgba(255, 107, 122, 0.3)`, text `#FF6B7A`.

### Transaction Chips & Category Pills

- Minimal footprint (`height: 32px`). Glass backdrop with `rounded-full` contour.
- Inactive state: Border `#262633`, text `#A1A1B5`.
- Active filter state: Border `#C6F432`, background `rgba(198, 244, 50, 0.08)`, text `#C6F432`.

### Transaction Lists

- Stacked modular rows separated by transparent gutters rather than native hairline dividers.
- Leading icon embedded within a frosted squircle (`#16161D`, 40x40px).
- Main merchant title in `body-md` (`#F8F9FA`), contextual auto-tag (e.g., "UPI • HDFC • 10:42 PM") in `body-sm` (`#A1A1B5`).
- Right-aligned trailing telemetry: `metric-mono-sm` colored `#FF6B7A` for debits, `#9FFFD0` for inflows.

### Input Fields & UPI Manual Overrides

- Height: 56px. Background: `#16161D` with subtle inset blur.
- Border: 1px `#262633`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#C6F432` with an ambient glow of `0 0 16px rgba(198, 244, 50, 0.15)`.
- Numeric input surfaces display massive `metric-mono-lg` figures with persistent Indian Rupee glyph (`₹`) styled in `#A1A1B5`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches

- Checkboxes use `rounded` (4px) geometric boxes. Checked state fills `#C6F432` with `#0B0B10` checkmark.
- Switches feature a wide capsule track (`#16161D`) with a 1px border (`#262633`). The sliding thumb uses an elevated frosted white pill, transitioning to vibrant `#C6F432` when active.

### Cards & Wealth Telemetry Widgets

- Composed of Elevation 2 Liquid Glass.
- Contains an integrated top-right refraction badge indicating sync status ("SMS AUTO-DETECTED", "UPI LIVE").
- Data charts within cards avoid harsh gridlines, opting for glowing vector gradients transitioning from `#C6F432` to zero alpha over the abyssal canvas.
